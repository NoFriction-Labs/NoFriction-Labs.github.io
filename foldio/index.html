What Foldio never does

  • Require an account
  • Upload your documents
  • Track your usage
  • Show you ads
  • Run analytics SDKs
  • Access the camera directly

Camera access is handled entirely by Google ML Kit — Foldio never declares the camera permission. Documents are stored in app-private internal storage and deleted when you uninstall. Full privacy policy →
