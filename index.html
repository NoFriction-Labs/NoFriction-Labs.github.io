The stance

Apps shouldn't work against you.

Somewhere along the way, software stopped being a tool you owned and became a meter that runs while you sleep. Subscriptions that never end. Ads that interrupt. Accounts you never wanted. Trackers you can't see.

NoFriction Labs is the opposite of all that. You pay once, you own it, your data is yours. The whole company fits in one promise: a tool should do its job and get out of your way.

What we stand for

Ten principles we build by.

Not a marketing list — the actual rules each app has to pass before it ships.

01

Private by design

No servers we operate, no analytics, no trackers. Your data lives on your device and never flows to us. Permissions are asked only when a feature needs them, with a plain reason first.

02

Offline by design

Core features run on-device, with no account and no constant connection. Some setup may need a one-time download — but once it's there, the work happens locally.

03

A genuinely useful free tier

The free version does real work and stands on its own. We don't hold core functionality hostage behind a trial or a timer.

04

Premium adds on top

A one-time purchase unlocks genuinely advanced capabilities layered on the free foundation. We never paywall the things that should've been free.

05

Pay once, own it forever

A single lifetime purchase — typically a few dollars. No recurring charges, no seats, no renewal anxiety. Ever.

06

No ads, no dark patterns

No banners, no interstitials, no engagement traps, no manipulative nags. Nothing competing for your attention but the task you opened the app to do.

07

Bring your own cloud

Optional sync runs through your own Google Drive (Dropbox / OneDrive / WebDAV-ready). Your data moves between your devices and your cloud — we're never in the middle.

08

Built on open source

We prefer well-maintained, openly-licensed libraries over proprietary black boxes — chosen per app, on the merits, not whatever's convenient.

09

Plays nicely with your phone

Real Android integration — share in, share out, import / export, deep links — so each app fits the way you already use your device.

10

Honest about reviews

We never bribe, beg, or gate features behind a rating. If you love an app you can say so — on your terms, never ours.
