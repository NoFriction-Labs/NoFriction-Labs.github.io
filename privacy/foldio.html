Privacy Policy — Foldio

Last updated: May 2026

Foldio is a document scanning app built by NoFriction Labs. This policy explains how your data is handled — the short version: we don't collect any.

Data We Collect

None. Foldio has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend servers that receive your data.

Your Documents

All scans, PDFs, and extracted text are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your files at any time.

Optional Cloud Backup

You may optionally connect your personal Google Drive account. If you do, documents are uploaded directly from your device to your own Drive using OAuth 2.0 with the drive.file scope — the narrowest available, meaning Foldio can only see files it created, never anything else in your Drive.

Foldio's servers are never involved. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings, which immediately revokes access.

Third-Party Services

Google ML Kit — Document scanning and text recognition (OCR) run entirely on-device. No images or text leave your device during these operations. Google Privacy Policy →

Google Drive — Only used if you explicitly connect it. Files transfer directly from your device to your account. Google Privacy Policy →

Google Play Billing — In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google Privacy Policy →

Permissions

Camera — Used only by the ML Kit document scanner. Images are processed on-device and never transmitted.

Storage — Used to save and read your documents locally on the device.

Internet — Only used if you enable optional Google Drive backup.

Children's Privacy

Foldio does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
