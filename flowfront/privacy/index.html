Privacy Policy — FlowFront

Effective October 2026 · NoFriction Labs · NoFrictionLabs@gmail.com

FlowFront is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What data FlowFront stores

DataUsed forLeaves your device?
Level progressRemembering which levels you finished and your best resultsNo
SettingsSound, vibration and similar preferencesNo

What FlowFront does NOT do

  • No account required. You never sign in.
  • No internet access. The app does not request the Android internet permission and never connects to a server.
  • No analytics. No crash reporting services, no usage tracking, no event logging.
  • No advertising. No ad SDKs, no ad identifiers, no behavioural profiling.
  • No third-party data sharing. There is nothing to share: no data is collected.

Purchases

FlowFront currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in the future, they will be handled entirely by Google Play Billing; FlowFront will never see or store your payment details. See Google's Payments Privacy Notice. This policy will be updated before any such change.

Data stored on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in a small file in app-private internal storage (/data/data/com.nofrictionlabs.flowfront/). This storage is:

  • Accessible only to FlowFront (Android enforces this at the OS level).
  • Deleted automatically when you uninstall the app.
  • Never sent to our servers or shared with other apps.

Children's Privacy

FlowFront is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Because the app collects no personal data from any user regardless of age, no special handling is required for younger users.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us at NoFrictionLabs@gmail.com and we will address it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
